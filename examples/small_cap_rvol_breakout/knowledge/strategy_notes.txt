# Strategy cheat sheet for agent knowledge grounding

Universe: small-cap equities under $20 with elevated relative volume and ATR%.

Preferred setup: long break of the first 5-minute opening-range high, only when
last price holds above VWAP and dollar volume / spread remain tradeable.

Risk: fixed fractional stop under the OR high; scale 50% at 1.5R; runner to 3R;
flatten on time stop or if price closes back inside the opening range.

This project is for education and research automation with CrewAI. It is not
personalized investment advice.
